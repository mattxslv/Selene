# Game details and Collection

## Details

![Details](../screenshots/details.png)

Details (from Spotlight, or X in Collection) shows who made the game, the critic and player scores, the genres as
chips, its platform with the store's mark and the description. Down from the pills goes into the bands below, and
the page glides up; the next band peeks in at the foot of the screen, blurred, until it's your turn to see it:

- **Screenshots** of the game (A shows one big).
- **Your captures** of it, with **Your activity** beside them: play time, last played, this week, your rank.
- **More like this**: games from your own collection by the same developer or with the same genres (A shows one on
  Spotlight).

Up goes back a band; B leaves the page.

## Collection

![Collection](../screenshots/library.png)

Playnite's full grid in Selene's look: the View button (or the Collection card, off by default) opens it. The game's
art crossfades behind the grid as you move, and a trailer plays when you rest on a game (switch: *Collection
trailers*; R3 for its sound). **A** plays the game the way Spotlight does, **X** opens its details, **Y** its options
(Play, Favorite, Hide, Edit fields, Uninstall...). *Start on* (Selene settings, Start-up) can open Playnite here instead of Spotlight, or on whichever of the two you were on last.
