## Sounds
`navigation.wav` (moving), `Boot.wav` (the intro), `Success.wav` (a profile made, a setup step done) and `Key.wav`
(typing, switches) are Selene's own, synthesized by `tools/make_sounds.py` (no samples).

`OpenPanel.wav` (a menu or panel opening), `LoginSelect.wav` (picking a profile), `GameStarting.wav` (pressing Play),
`GameReady.wav` (the game is up), `activation.wav` (confirm), `CloseAdditionalView.wav` (back / close) and `Alert.wav`
(asking before something can't be undone) are menu sounds from SQUARE ENIX games (FINAL FANTASY VII REMAKE, FINAL
FANTASY XVI, CRISIS CORE, KINGDOM HEARTS III), (c) SQUARE ENIX. They are not free to reuse: they are here by the
maintainer's choice and will be replaced if asked.

## Background music
`theme/Media/Selene OST.mp3` is the login music (`audio/LoginOST.mp3`) of the Aniki ReMake Playnite theme by Mike Aniki
(https://github.com/Mike-Aniki/Aniki-ReMake, MIT License). It is the default music Selene sets in UniPlaySong when no default file is
chosen. The file's own tag reads "UI#12 (UI/UI058_Equip_Off)" (2008) and Aniki ReMake does not say where the audio comes from, so it may
not be Mike Aniki's to license: it is here by the maintainer's choice and will be removed or replaced if he or a rights holder asks
(the swap is one file).

## Add-ons installed by the setup
Selene's first-launch setup installs these Playnite add-ons through Playnite's own installer; they are not bundled and
keep their own licenses: Extra Metadata Loader (darklinkpower), Ludusavi (mtkennerly), HowLongToBeat and GameActivity
(Lacro59), Playnite Achievements (justin-delano), DuplicateHider (felixkmh), UniPlaySong (aHuddini) and Audio Switcher
(Naerian). For save backups it also downloads the [Ludusavi](https://github.com/mtkennerly/ludusavi) program
(MIT License, Copyright (c) 2020 Matthew T. Kennerly) into Selene Core's data folder.
For game songs it downloads [yt-dlp](https://github.com/yt-dlp/yt-dlp) (The Unlicense) and an
[ffmpeg](https://ffmpeg.org) build from [gyan.dev](https://www.gyan.dev/ffmpeg/builds/) (GPL v3) the same way, for
UniPlaySong to use; neither is bundled.
