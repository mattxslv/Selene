# Captures, Activity, Surprise me

Each card shows a headline and a strip of pictures under it (the latest captures, the games you played last, a few
ideas). Down goes into the strip, A opens the picture. Each card has a switch under **Spotlight** in Selene
settings; *Pictures under cards* switches the strips.

## Captures

![Captures](../screenshots/captures.png)
Your screenshots and clips, newest first, from Steam, Xbox Game Bar (`Videos\Captures`), the NVIDIA app
(`Videos\NVIDIA`), `Videos\<game>` folders and any folder you add on Selene Core's add-on page. The grid sits on a dark
vignette with the focused capture glowing behind it; LB / RB filter to screenshots or clips.

In the viewer: **A** pauses and plays a clip, **Left / Right** go 10 seconds, **LB / RB** go to the previous or
next capture, **B** closes. Clips play with their sound. Clips Playnite's own player can't decode (the NVIDIA app's
AV1 recordings) play in the browser Playnite ships with. Clips get stills from Windows' own thumbnails, or from
ffmpeg when this PC has it.

## Activity

![Activity](../screenshots/activity.png)

What you've been playing: this week's and all-time numbers as cards, your top game with its share of your play time,
**Most played** with bars, **Played last**, **Pick up again** (an hour or more played, untouched for 45 days) and
**Still waiting** (never played). A shows a game on Spotlight, X plays it. Play time comes from your profile, with
Playnite's own numbers for games the profile hasn't played yet.

## Surprise me
Covers flick past and stop on a game to play. LB / RB (or *Surprise me games* in settings) choose the pool:
installed, not played yet, or all. A plays it, X shows it on Spotlight.
