# Code of conduct

Selene's issues and discussions are for people who like games and want a nicer Playnite. Keep it friendly.

- Be kind and patient, especially with people new to Playnite or GitHub.
- Disagree with ideas, not people. No insults, harassment, slurs or personal attacks.
- No spam, ads, piracy links or requests, and no sharing other people's private information.
- Stay on topic: bugs in Issues, ideas and questions in Discussions.

Comments that break these rules may be edited, hidden or removed, and repeat or serious cases can be blocked from the repository.

If someone is making the space unpleasant, report the comment with GitHub's **Report content** option, or reach the maintainer privately through [Report a vulnerability](https://github.com/mattxslv/Selene/security/advisories/new) if it's serious.

Based on the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/).
